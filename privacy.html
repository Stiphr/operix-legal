Privacy Policy

Stiphr Design · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains what the Stiphr Design Service and Stiphr Broker Desk Discord apps collect, why, and what you can do about it. Stiphr Design (“we”, “us”) runs both apps.

1. What we collect

  • Discord profile: your user ID, username, display name and avatar link.
  • Messages: the text and attachment links of messages you send in ticket channels or by direct message to the Stiphr Design Service bot, and archived transcripts of ticket conversations.
  • Orders: quotes, prices, order status and history, revision requests, delivery records, and staff notes about your order.
  • Payments: transaction hashes, the addresses we give you, the network and the amounts.
  • Invoices: the details needed to issue your invoice, including your email address if you give it to us.
  • Stiphr Broker Desk: sales, stock and settlement records entered by its authorised users, including any buyer names, references and notes they add. Message content is not collected by this app.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to run your tickets, quote and deliver orders, confirm payments, issue invoices, keep accounting records, prevent duplicate or fraudulent payments, and keep the apps secure. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who else handles it

  • Discord carries all messages and interactions, under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram: ticket messages and direct messages to the bot can be copied to our private staff chat so that staff can reply.
  • Gofile hosts delivery files. Anyone who has a delivery link can open that file.
  • Blockchain data services receive transaction hashes and addresses so that we can check payments. Blockchains are public, so transactions on them can be seen by anyone and cannot be deleted by us.

Our order, invoice and accounting systems run on a private server that we operate. We may also share information when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order, payment and invoice records for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute handling. Ticket transcripts and client profiles are kept until they are no longer needed or you ask us to delete them, unless we must keep them. Deleted information can remain in encrypted or private backups until those backups expire.

5. Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We may need to keep financial records that the law requires. Information already on a public blockchain or held by Discord is outside our control.

6. Security

We limit access to authorised staff and keep the data on a private server with access controls and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords, recovery phrases or private keys in a ticket. We will never ask for them.

7. Children

The apps are not meant for anyone under the minimum age for Discord in their country.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Open a ticket in the Stiphr Design Discord server, or send a direct message to the Stiphr Design Service bot.
